A tiny field trip for everyone

Curiosity can start with one animal.

Animal Generator is a small, free animal generator for curious minds. Pick one animal to spark a sketch, choose a few for a classroom game, or simply meet a species you haven’t thought about today.

Why we made it

A little surprise makes room for a big idea.

Creative work often begins with a prompt. We wanted a simple way to find one without signing up, sorting through menus, or needing to know what to search for first.

So we made a lightweight picker that brings one unexpected animal to the page. Use it however your curiosity takes you: draw it, learn about it, invent a story, or pass it along as a game prompt.

✳

Simple by design

Choose from 1 to 10 animals and get a result right away. No account or setup needed.

Made for making
