# Mammal full-body photo review

Reviewed all 109 existing mammal source images visually on 2026-11-04. The contact sheets contained at most 12 images, with individual image areas at least 320 by 220 pixels. Borderline cases were enlarged and rechecked.

Initial result: 71 original photographs pass; 38 need replacement. European wildcat passes: the source includes the head, body, paws and curled tail; the reported page clipping comes from the display crop rather than the photograph.

A pass requires a clear, complete primary animal, without original image edges cutting its head, torso, visible tail or limbs. Natural sitting postures, folded limbs, and limbs behind the torso are acceptable. Heavy vegetation, overlapping subjects hiding much of the body, portraits and partially surfaced marine mammals fail.

Replacement research uses actual Wikimedia Commons API imageinfo and extmetadata records for the file page, author and reuse license. Candidate images are visually checked before being accepted. WebP conversion preserves the complete source frame without cropping or artificial enlargement.

Download limitation: Wikimedia upload image service returned HTTP 429 with Retry-After 600 during this review. API and thumbnail downloads are intermittent. No candidate is marked as a replacement until its image is downloaded and visually verified.
