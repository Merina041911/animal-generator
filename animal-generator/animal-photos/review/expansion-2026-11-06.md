# Expansion to 300 species (2026-11-06)

This batch adds 60 available species: 54 new catalog birds and 6 existing catalog species with newly researched filter metadata and approved photos. The catalog now contains 394 species: 319 have researched metadata, 300 have approved photographs, 19 still await replacement photos, and 75 await filter research. All 240 previously enabled records remain unchanged.

## Review method

Candidates were checked individually for species identity, a clear complete main-body outline, source-frame clipping, and severe obstruction. Floating birds may naturally have feet underwater; folded wings and single-leg resting poses retain their natural appearance. Head-only photographs, unrelated predators or prey, and cropped or severely obscured subjects were excluded. Images retain their original composition, are reduced proportionally and converted to WebP without upscaling.

Photo reuse is limited to CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA, with photographer and license attribution retained individually. Research mainly uses Animal Diversity Web; common pochard also uses BirdLife International and The Wildlife Trusts. Per-species notes retain historical names, taxonomic splits and source limitations. Size uses body length rather than wingspan.
