# Aquatic and invertebrate full-body photo review

Reviewed on 2026-11-04. All 72 original images were visually inspected in six contact sheets (12 per sheet; image cells at least 320 × 220 px). No existing manual-review label was used as a substitute for visual inspection.

## Standard

The head, trunk, visible limbs and tail/fins/tentacles must fit inside the original image and remain clearly recognizable. Natural curling and far-side limbs may be hidden by the animal itself. Photographic edge cuts, serious environmental occlusion and indistinct or blurred views fail. Illustrations and generated images are excluded.

- Original images needing replacement: 45.
- Visually verified replacements downloaded: 13.
- Research is still in progress; entries without a `replacement` are unresolved and must not be represented as compliant full-body photographs.

## Source and resolution notes

Each replacement records its exact Commons file page, artist and commercial-use license from the Commons imageinfo API. Downloaded thumbnails are used where originals returned HTTP 429 or transient TLS connection failures. Their actual output dimensions are recorded without upscaling. The 687 px pseudoscorpion is a clear microscope photograph; some replacement photos are 960 px wide. Higher-resolution originals remain preferable when the server is available.

Original-image contact sheets and candidate evidence are in `/tmp/aquatic-fullbody/` for this local research session; the JSON is the durable per-animal review.
