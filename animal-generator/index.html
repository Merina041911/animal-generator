How filters work

Select any options within a group; an animal only needs to match one. Across different groups, it must match every selected group. Leave a group unselected to include everything.

Size uses adult body length: small under 10 cm, medium from 10 cm to under 1 m, and large from 1 m. Mammals use head–body length and birds exclude wingspan; reptiles and fish use total length. Species can appear in more than one size band; invertebrate measurements are explained in the species notes. Ocean & coast includes shore habitats. Region refers to native wild ranges, including migration, with domestic distributions noted separately.

Random animal generator FAQ

Answers about animal types, pictures, quantities, and using your random animal idea.

What is a random animal generator?

It is a simple animal picker that chooses a species at random. Use it to discover an animal, start a drawing prompt, or choose an idea for a classroom activity or game.

Can I generate 1 to 10 random animals at once?

Yes. Choose any number from 1 to 10 in the quantity menu, then select Generate animals. The results appear together so you can compare or use them as a group prompt.

Can I filter animals by type, habitat, diet, size or region?

Yes. Open any filter and select one or more options. Selections within a group are combined, and different groups narrow the results together. The toolbar shows how many photo-backed species match. Reset all filters to return to the full collection.

Will I see the same animal again?

New generations prioritize animals you have not seen. After all matches for your current filters have been used, a fresh round starts. A batch never includes the same species twice; if fewer species match than the number requested, you receive all available matches.

How many animal species are in this animal generator?

The catalog contains 394 species across mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, and invertebrates. You can currently generate 300 species with reviewed photos; animals without an approved photo stay out of the random results.

What kinds of animals can this animal generator pick?

The collection includes mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, and invertebrates such as insects, molluscs, crustaceans, arachnids, echinoderms, and cnidarians.

Does this random animal generator show animal photos?

Yes. Every result currently includes a reviewed photo. There are 300 photo-backed species in the generator, with images sourced from Wikimedia Commons and iNaturalist. Creators, licenses, and source pages are listed on the image credits page.

Can I use the random animal picker for drawing prompts?

Yes. Generate one animal for a quick sketch, or choose several to combine into a character, story, or creative challenge.

Is this free random animal generator available without signing up?

Yes. You can use the generator without creating an account or installing an app.

Is the animal generator useful for kids and classroom activities?

Yes. Teachers, parents, and kids can use a random animal as a quick learning topic, drawing prompt, or guessing game. The tool does not require an account.

Can I use the animal generator on my phone?

Yes. The animal cards and quantity selector adjust to smaller screens, including when you generate several animals at once.
