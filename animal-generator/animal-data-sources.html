How the filters are defined

  • Habitat: broad environments a species regularly uses. Multiple labels can apply. Urban & farmland includes human-modified settings.
  • Diet: common adult diet. Insect-eaters are carnivores; mixed plant and animal diets are omnivorous. Detritivores eat organic debris. Non-feeding adults are listed separately instead of inheriting their larval diet.
  • Size: small under 10 cm; medium from 10 cm to under 1 m; large from 1 m. We use adult head–body length for land mammals, body length rather than wingspan for birds, and total length for reptiles and fish. Invertebrates use body length or the documented shell/bell diameter, with exceptions in species notes. Ranges that cross a threshold can appear in multiple bands.
  • Region: native wild ranges, including migration; Central America and Greenland count as North America. Introduced populations are generally excluded. Domestic distributions and uncertain origins are identified in species notes. World oceans is a marine range label, not a continent.
  • Evidence: source pages below support each species record. Tags summarize those accounts; they are editorial mappings rather than direct quotations. Historic taxonomy, source discrepancies and uncertainty are retained in the notes. Uncertain fields match only when that filter group is left unrestricted.
